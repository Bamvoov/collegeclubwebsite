Why We Exist

We are the Journalism Club VITB, and we refuse to let the stories of our campus fade into background noise. We exist to uncover what is hidden, to question the accepted, and to broadcast the unfiltered truth of student life.

In a world saturated with carefully curated PR and endless noise, genuine voices are easily drowned out. We believe that every student, every event, and every controversy deserves to be examined with integrity and raw honesty. Our mission is not just to report the news, but to spark conversations that matter.

> TRUTH IS NOT A LUXURY. IT IS A NECESSITY.

Whether we are writing articles, capturing raw photojournalism, or hosting underground interviews, our goal remains the same: to empower our community with knowledge. We are the pulse of VITB. We are the voice of the unspoken. We are here to stay.
